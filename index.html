About me

I am an economist working on questions in fertility and family economics. My dissertation, “Essays Analyzing Family Policy and Trade Utilizing the Synthetic Control Method,” studies how policy interventions reshape family formation and international trade, using the synthetic control method to construct data-driven counterfactuals from historical and cross-national data.

I expect to complete my Ph.D. in May 2027. I am available for interviews throughout the 2026–2027 academic job-market season.
