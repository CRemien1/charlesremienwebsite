Teaching

Courses and Teaching Philosophy

My teaching emphasizes the hidden economics of everyday life, from the smallest consumer decisions to the economic trajectory of entire nations. Using the thinking skills I teach like a magnifying glass, students leaving my classes should have the skills necessary to discover hidden truths themselves.
